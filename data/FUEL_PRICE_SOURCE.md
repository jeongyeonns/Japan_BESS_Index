# Fuel price comparison data

`fuel_prices_2026.csv` contains monthly nominal prices from the World Bank Commodity Price Data (Pink Sheet), downloaded from the official monthly workbook:

<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/74e8be41ceb20fa0da750cda2f6b9e4e-0050012026/related/CMO-Historical-Data-Monthly.xlsx>

The Japan LNG series is `Liquefied natural gas, Japan` in USD/MMBtu. The coal series is `Coal, Australian` in USD/metric tonne. JKM is intentionally blank because a stable, reusable public JKM spot or futures time series was not verified. The app does not substitute another LNG benchmark.
